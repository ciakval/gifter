<objective>
Members of an event can talk privately about someone's wish, and about the person in general, on dedicated thread pages reached from the event page. The wish owner and the person themselves get a plain 404 everywhere those pages live.

Purpose: DISC-01 and DISC-02 (D-01, D-02). This plan builds the data model, the single chokepoint module, both thread kinds, the gate, the entry links and the Czech strings. "New since your last visit" markers, deleting a comment, the privacy policy and the full secrecy suite follow in 05-02 and 05-03. The phase ships as one deploy after all three plans; do not push or deploy after this plan alone, because the privacy policy (05-02) and the exhaustive secrecy suite (05-03) are part of the promise.

Output: three additive tables, `gifter/discussions.py`, two views and URLs, `discussion.html`, links on the event page, probes in the secrecy suite's route list, Czech catalogue entries.
</objective>

<decisions>
There is no CONTEXT.md. The locked decisions come from the ROADMAP Phase 5 Notes (brainstorm of 2026-11-14) and are numbered here for traceability. Planner choices that were left open are marked P-.

- D-01: A thread belongs to a wish within one event. The same wish in another event has a separate thread.
- D-02: A user-level discussion per (giftee, event) is included. It is open to every member of the event except that person.
- D-03: An author can delete their own comment. Nobody can edit a comment (no edit route, no updated_at column).
- D-04: A "new since your last visit" marker, no notifications and no emails. (Built in 05-02.)
- D-05: The giftee must never learn that a thread or comment exists: page, count, marker, URL, POST response or admin. Comment text joins the secrecy suite's sentinels. (Proven in 05-03.)
- D-06: The privacy policy is updated in English and Czech. (05-02.)
- D-07: `delete_user` removes a person's comments. (05-03.)
- D-08: Every new string is translated to Czech and the catalogue gate passes.
- P-01 (discretion): threads live on dedicated pages reached from the event page, not inline, so the event page stays short on phones and event_detail never loads comment text.
- P-02 (discretion): comment body is plain text, 1 to 1000 characters, rendered escaped with line breaks. No Markdown, no links turned into anchors, no mentions.
- P-03 (discretion): all new foreign keys use related_name "+". No accessor then exists on Wish, Event or User, so an owner template cannot reach thread data by accident (same device as Claim.wish).
- P-04 (discretion): plain POST then redirect, like the claim actions. The app has no htmx templates today, so none is introduced.
</decisions>
