# Plan 01-03: Relatives who open Gifter inside a chat app are told how to get in

## Phase Goal

> ROADMAP `**Goal:**` (verbatim, not in user-story format): "Anyone with a Google account can sign in to Gifter at https://gifter.remesh.cz. The site runs on a cleaned-up Django 6.1 codebase that redeploys automatically from `main`." (MVP story not invented; run `/gsd-mvp-phase 1` to reframe.)

This plan was split out of plan 01-02 (revision for the per-plan file budget). Its content is the former 01-02 Task 3, unchanged in scope, now in two user-visible steps.

<objective>
Expand the proven sign-in slice for relatives who open the Gifter link inside Messenger, Instagram or a similar in-app browser, where Google refuses to sign anyone in: (1) instead of the Google button they see a plain-language "open this in your browser" hint with numbered steps, the page link ready to copy by hand and a Continue anyway escape; (2) a Copy link button copies that link with one tap.

Purpose: OPS-04 and success criterion 4 (Claude's discretion per CONTEXT: detection scope, interstitial vs banner, copy-link vs deep link). The landing page keeps the D-01 name and one sentence, the normal branch keeps the D-03 POST form with the D-04 hidden next, and every string is English and wrapped (D-05).
Output: gifter/inapp.py, an in_app branch in the landing view and landing.html, gifter/static/gifter/landing.js, gifter/tests/test_inapp.py.
</objective>
