# Plan 01-07: Google is the only door, enforced (WR-03, WR-04, WR-05)

## Phase Goal

> ROADMAP `**Goal:**` (verbatim, not in user-story format): "Anyone with a Google account can sign in to Gifter at https://gifter.remesh.cz. The site runs on a cleaned-up Django 6.1 codebase that redeploys automatically from `main`." (No user story invented.)

Gap-closure plan 2 of 3. The user accepted the code-review recommendation to fix WR-03, WR-04 and WR-05 before relatives are invited. WR-01 is fixed in 01-06; WR-02 is partly fixed there (action SHA pins, permissions) and recorded as deferred for the secrets: inherit / @v1 remainder. WR-06, WR-07 and IN-01..IN-08 are explicitly out of scope; this plan only records them as deferred. Runs after 01-06 because both plans edit web/settings.py, DEPLOYMENT.md and scripts/smoke.sh.

<objective>
Replace the wholesale allauth URL include with an explicit allowlist of the routes Google-only sign-in needs. Add two small allauth adapters, so the routes allauth would otherwise reverse into (inactive account, account connect) cannot cause a 500. Lock the admin down to read-only Google identity data, without the SocialApp/SocialToken pages and without any wish or claim models. Make promote_staff resolve people through a Google-verified email and print the Google uid. Prove all of it with tests and live smoke checks, and record the review dispositions.

Purpose: AUTH-01 "Google is the only login method" is enforced in the URL table, not only by convention (COVERAGE.md already opts out of One Tap, connected-accounts UI and account linking). AUTH-03: the login redirect keeps `next` (D-04).
Output: web/urls.py allowlist, gifter/adapters.py, admin lockdown, promote_staff rewrite, tests, smoke checks (j) and (k), DEPLOYMENT.md, review disposition ledger.
</objective>
