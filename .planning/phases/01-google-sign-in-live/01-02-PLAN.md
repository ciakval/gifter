# Plan 01-02: Google is the only door, and its dead ends lead back to sign-in

## Phase Goal

> ROADMAP `**Goal:**` (verbatim, not in user-story format): "Anyone with a Google account can sign in to Gifter at https://gifter.remesh.cz. The site runs on a cleaned-up Django 6.1 codebase that redeploys automatically from `main`." (MVP story not invented; run `/gsd-mvp-phase 1` to reframe.)

<objective>
Expand the proven sign-in slice in two user-visible steps: (1) every other way in is closed or redirected to the Google landing page, and the author can reach a Google-only admin; (2) allauth's own pages (sign-out confirmation, sign-in error, cancel) look like Gifter and lead back to sign-in.

Purpose: AUTH-01 (Google only, nothing else), AUTH-03 (redirects keep next, D-04). Strings stay English and wrapped (D-05). The in-app browser hint (OPS-04) is plan 01-03, which runs in the same wave.
Output: updated web/urls.py, admin registration, promote_staff command, three allauth template overrides, two test modules.
</objective>

## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| browser → Django | Untrusted requests (with their next query strings and form bodies) reach accounts/login/, gifter-admin/login/ and allauth's pages |
| operator shell → DB | promote_staff runs with full DB rights inside the container |
| anonymous → admin | /gifter-admin/ must only open for staff signed in with Google |
