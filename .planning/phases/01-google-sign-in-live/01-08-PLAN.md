# Plan 01-08: Deploy the privacy page and lockdown, publish the consent screen, prove sign-in for any Google account

## Phase Goal

> ROADMAP `**Goal:**` (verbatim, not in user-story format): "Anyone with a Google account can sign in to Gifter at https://gifter.remesh.cz. The site runs on a cleaned-up Django 6.1 codebase that redeploys automatically from `main`." (No user story invented.)

Gap-closure plan 3 of 3. It closes the blocking gap in 01-VERIFICATION.md: the consent screen is in Testing, so only listed test users can sign in. It also closes the four human-verification items that 01-05 only covered by a blanket approval. Same operating model as 01-05: Claude verifies from outside (gh, curl, smoke.sh). The user does everything on Bombur and in Google Cloud Console.

<objective>

Finally, verify with a Google account that was never a test user, and capture the human checks one by one: session survives the redeploys, browser restart and sign-out, phone layout, real Messenger and Instagram wording.

Purpose: ROADMAP SC-2 and the phase goal ("anyone with a Google account"); AUTH-01 re-confirmed for arbitrary accounts; AUTH-02 and OPS-04 checks that were never individually reported. This also mitigates the Testing-status threat registered in plan 01-05.
Output: a live deploy of 01-06 and 01-07; a published consent screen; a SUMMARY with each human check's result; REQUIREMENTS.md and STATE.md updated.
</objective>
