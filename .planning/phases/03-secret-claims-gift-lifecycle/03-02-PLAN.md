# Plan 03-02: Received flow, secrecy test suite and privacy update

## Phase Goal

> ROADMAP `**Goal:**` (verbatim, not in user-story format): "Members can coordinate who buys what (claim, chip in, mark purchased) and giftees can close wishes once received. A giftee never learns who claimed or contributed, and automated tests enforce this." No user story is invented here. Run `/gsd-mvp-phase 3` if you want one.

This is plan 2 of 2 in Phase 3. It runs after 03-01 because it builds on the Claim and Contribution models, the My claims page and the claim URLs, and both plans edit views.py, urls.py and owner/participant templates. When it is done, the phase goal is complete: giftees close wishes as received, and an automated suite proves on every deploy that the giftee cannot tell whether their wishes are claimed.

<objective>
First, the giftee marks a wish received: it leaves every event page, moves into a collapsed Received section and can be reopened, while claimers still see "Received" on My claims. Second, the secrecy suite: a full-resolver route inventory guard, a sentinel crawl of every route as the giftee, a claim-state check scoped to the giftee's own-wish regions, byte-identical snapshots with and without claims, a uniform-404 matrix, owner-view query capture, and a self-sensitivity test that injects a leak on every run. Third, the privacy policy describes claims and chip-ins, guarded by the date gate, and the smoke script checks /claims/.

Purpose: LIFE-01..LIFE-03, SECR-02 and the proof of SECR-01. Covers decisions D-09, D-10, D-11, D-12, D-13, D-14, D-15, D-16 and D-17.
Output: received/reopen views and templates, gifter/tests/test_received.py, gifter/tests/test_secrecy.py, the privacy section and constant bump, smoke check (n) and the DEPLOYMENT.md privacy rule.
</objective>

## Assumptions (flagged)

- **A-06 (SECR-01, unclassified):** The secrecy boundary is the wish owner, per wish, not a per-event role.
