# Plan 03-01: Secret claims, chip-in and My claims

## Phase Goal

> ROADMAP `**Goal:**` (verbatim, not in user-story format): "Members can coordinate who buys what (claim, chip in, mark purchased) and giftees can close wishes once received. A giftee never learns who claimed or contributed, and automated tests enforce this." No user story is invented here. Run `/gsd-mvp-phase 3` if you want one.

This is plan 1 of 2 in Phase 3 and delivers the participant side. Members can claim, chip in, mark purchased and release, from the event page and from a My claims page, with changed and withdrawn notices, and the giftee's own pages never change. Plan 03-02 adds the received flow, the full secrecy test suite (sentinel crawl, byte diff of every route, 404 matrix) and the privacy update. Both plans edit views.py and urls.py, so they run one after the other.

<objective>
Add the Claim and Contribution tables, in one additive migration together with the received status. Then deliver claims as vertical slices. First, a member claims a wish, everyone except the giftee sees it as taken (names only for people who share an event with the claimer), and the race loser sees "Someone just took it". Second, members chip in with a note, and the claimer marks the claim purchased or releases it with a hand-over. Third, My claims lists everything across events, including un-shared, withdrawn and received wishes and the "changed since you claimed" marker.

Purpose: CLAM-02..CLAM-08 and the structural half of SECR-01. Covers decisions D-01, D-02, D-03, D-04, D-05, D-06, D-07, D-08, D-10 (My claims display) and D-13/D-15 (early guards; plan 03-02 adds the full suite).
Output: migration 0004, gifter/claims.py (secrecy chokepoint plus services), gifter/claim_views.py, participant card UI, My claims page and nav link, tests.
</objective>

## Assumption-delta decision

<assumption_delta_decision>
The detector fired: pluralization on "the second sees someone just took it" and "can also reopen it", and optional on "an optional note". Primary noun: the Claim row, at most one per wish (OneToOne). It is what "taken" means, it belongs to the wish rather than to an event (D-05), and contributions hang off it. Decision: no-change. A second claimer is rejected by the unique constraint, not stored alongside the first. On release, the hand-over (D-06) changes the claimer on the same row, so the identity of the claim never forks. The optional note is a detail of a Contribution, not a new anchor. Reopen (plan 03-02) only toggles Wish.status. Invariant test, added in Task 1 and extended in Task 2: after every path (claim, simultaneous claims, chip-in, release with and without contributors) there is at most one Claim row per wish, and its claimer is never the wish owner.
</assumption_delta_decision>

- **A-02 (CLAM-05, unclassified):** "Earliest contributor" is ordered by chip-in time (created_at, then id). The hand-over resets the status to reserved and carries over the new claimer's acknowledgement time. No notification is sent (v1 has none); the new claimer finds it on My claims.
- **A-03 (CLAM-06, unclassified):** Purchased is one-way in this phase (there is no un-mark). Every non-giftee viewer sees it as a badge, with names per D-02. Releasing a purchased claim hands over as reserved.
- **A-04 (CLAM-07, unclassified):** My claims lists all of the viewer's claims and contributions, whatever the current sharing. The order is active, withdrawn, received, newest involvement first within each group. Each item shows the wish details and the owner's display name.
- **A-05 (CLAM-08, unclassified):** "Edited" means the owner saved a change to the name, description, priority or links. Share-only saves, received/reopen (plan 03-02) and withdrawal do not raise the marker; withdrawal shows "Withdrawn by the owner" instead.

Prohibition probe (spec-less fallback): four bespoke prohibitions are recorded in `must_haves.prohibitions`, without descriptors. Canon breadcrumbs, not minted here: CSRF on state-changing endpoints and open redirects via `next` are OWASP canon, mitigated in the threat model (T-03-05, T-03-07) and covered by /gsd-secure-phase.
