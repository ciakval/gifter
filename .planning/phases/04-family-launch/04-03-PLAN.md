# Plan 04-03: A truthful privacy policy in both languages, and the secrecy suite in Czech

## Phase Goal

> ROADMAP `**Goal:**` (verbatim, not in user-story format): "Czech- and English-speaking relatives can use Gifter comfortably on their phones, the data is safely backed up, and a real family dry-run has passed before the 1 Dec freeze." No user story is invented here. Run `/gsd-mvp-phase 4` if you want one.

This plan finishes the language half of the goal (I18N-01) that plan 04-01 started: the privacy page covers the language cookie, one-off copies and (from the first nightly backup on, never before) the nightly backups, in English and Czech, and the whole secrecy suite passes a second time in Czech. It runs in wave 2 after plan 04-01 (switch, complete catalogue, catalogue gate, set_language probes) and plan 04-02 (gifter-backup.sh, which writes the marker this plan reads). Plans 04-04 (phone pass) and 04-05 (health check, launch hardening, dry-run kit) follow in wave 3. No checkpoints; nothing is pushed or deployed by this plan. Once it is complete, nothing committed so far leaves /privacy/ or the secrecy suite behind the code.

<objective>
Keep the privacy policy truthful in both languages about the new language cookie, about one-off maintenance copies, and about nightly backups exactly from the moment the first nightly backup exists; and prove that the secrecy guarantees hold in Czech too.

Purpose: relatives rely on the policy, and the core promise (the giftee never learns about claims) must survive translation.
Output: privacy view reading the nightly-backups-since marker, privacy.html with always-shown and gated sentences, translated and compiled catalogue, privacy tests with a temporary DATA_DIR, date gate, Czech pass of the secrecy suite with a Czech-really guard.
</objective>
