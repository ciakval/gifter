{% translate "Privacy policy" %}

{% translate "Gifter is a small, non-commercial website for family and friends to coordinate gifts. It is run privately by the person who set it up (the site operator). This page explains what Gifter stores about you today and why." %}

{% translate "What Gifter stores" %}

{% translate "When you sign in with Google, Google tells Gifter your Google account ID, your name, your email address (and whether Google has verified it) and the web address of your Google profile picture. Gifter keeps these to recognise you when you come back. It does not show your profile picture anywhere." %}

{% translate "Gifter also records when you joined and when you last signed in." %}

{% translate "Events" %}

{% translate "When you create or join an event, Gifter stores the event's name, date and description, who created it, who its members are and when they joined. Every event also has an invite link." %}

{% translate "Wishes" %}

{% translate "Gifter stores the wishes you add: name, description, priority, links, when you added and last changed them, and which events you share each one into." %}

{% translate "When you delete a wish it disappears from your wishlist and from every event page, but Gifter keeps a copy in its database. To have it erased, use the data request described under Keeping and deleting your data below." %}

{% translate "Claims and chip-ins" %}

{% translate "When you say you will get someone else's wish, mark it as purchased, release it, or chip in on someone else's claim with an optional note, Gifter stores who did it, when, and the note." %}

{% translate "The person whose wish it is never sees any of this: not who claimed it or chipped in, not the notes, and not even whether anyone did." %}

{% translate "Other members who can see the wish see that it is taken or purchased. The names of the people who claimed it or chipped in, and their notes, are shown only to members who share at least one event with them; everyone else sees “someone”." %}

{% translate "When the owner marks a wish as received, Gifter stores that. Claims and chip-ins on received or deleted wishes stay in Gifter's database. To have them erased, use the data request described under Keeping and deleting your data below." %}

{% translate "Before Gifter adds a feature that stores more about you, this page will be updated first to say what it stores and who can see it." %}

{% translate "Discussions" %}

{% translate "When you write a comment on someone else's wish, or in the discussion about a person, inside an event, Gifter stores the text, who wrote it, when, and which event and wish or person it belongs to. Gifter also remembers when you last opened each discussion, so it can show you what is new. Gifter sends no notifications or emails about comments." %}

{% translate "The person whose wish it is, and the person a discussion is about, never sees the discussion: not the comments, not how many there are, and not even that the discussion exists. Every other member of the event can read it and sees who wrote each comment." %}

{% translate "You can delete your own comments at any time, and they are then removed from Gifter's database. Nobody can edit a comment. Discussions on wishes that are deleted or no longer shared stay in Gifter's database but are no longer shown. To have them erased, use the data request described under Keeping and deleting your data below." %}

{% translate "What Gifter does not get" %}

{% translate "Gifter never sees your Google password. It asks Google only for your basic profile and email address, never for your contacts, files, calendar or anything else, and it does not keep any Google access tokens." %}

{% translate "Cookies" %}

{% translate "Gifter uses only the cookies it needs to work: one keeps you signed in for up to 90 days, one protects forms against forgery, short-lived ones carry one-time notices, and one remembers the language you picked with the EN / CS switch, for up to a year. There are no advertising or tracking cookies." %}

{% translate "Who can see your information" %}

{% translate "Your Google account details are visible only to you and the site operator." %}

{% translate "Every member of an event can see the event's details, the names of the other members (the name from their Google account, never their email address) and the event's invite link. Anyone who has the invite link can join the event after signing in, so share it only with people you want in the event. The site operator can see everything Gifter stores." %}

{% translate "Every member of an event can see the wishes you share into that event, until you mark them as received or delete them. A wish you have never shared into any event is seen only by you." %}

{% translate "Anyone who is getting one of your wishes or chipping in on it keeps seeing it in their My claims, even after you stop sharing it, mark it as received or delete it. Who can see claims and chip-ins is described under Claims and chip-ins above." %}

{% translate "Gifter never sells or shares your data with anyone. There is no advertising and no analytics, and Gifter's pages load no scripts, fonts or images from other companies." %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %}Signing in is handled by Google, under the Google Privacy Policy.{% endblocktranslate %}

{% translate "Your data is stored on a server in the European Union that the site operator runs. The server may keep short-lived technical logs (time, page requested and IP address) to keep Gifter running and secure." %}

{% translate "Before maintenance, such as erasing an account or restoring a backup, the site operator may make a one-off copy of Gifter's database on the same server, and deletes it by hand once the work has been checked." %}
